# Privacy Policy

Last updated: `2026-10-22`

`LTO Theory` is an offline study app for the Philippine LTO non-professional driver's license theory exam.

**Public URL (use this in Google Play):**  
https://stasholmansky.github.io/lto-theory-privacy/

## Who we are

For questions about this policy, contact:

- Email: `stanislavkholmanskii@gmail.com`

## What the app stores

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

- Study position, so learning can resume later
- Questions you missed while studying
- Language preference
- Appearance preference (system / light / dark)
- Whether the language picker on first launch has been completed

The app does not create an account and does not upload this information.

## Network requests

The questions, translations, and road-sign explanations are included in the app. The app does not contact our servers or third-party services for content, accounts, or analytics.

The Android manifest includes the internet permission because the React Native framework declares it. This version of the app does not send or receive user data over the network.

Feedback is sent only if you choose to write an email in your own mail app.

## Accounts and authentication

The app does not require an account and does not include sign-in.

## Analytics, ads, and tracking

The app does not include advertising SDKs or analytics/tracking SDKs.

## Location

The app does not request location permission.

## Data sharing

Study progress is not shared with other users. It stays on your device unless you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

## Data retention

Your data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

## Your choices

You can:

- Change language and appearance at any time
- Remove app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage
- Contact `stanislavkholmanskii@gmail.com` with a privacy question
