# Store Listing Draft

## Product Positioning

`LTO Theory` is an offline, multilingual practice app for the Philippine LTO non-professional driver's license theory exam. The questions are approximate practice items collected from official and unofficial sources. They help you prepare. They are not the exact exam and do not replace the official theory.

## Full Description

```
Prepare for the Philippine LTO non-professional theory exam offline, in several languages.

These are not the exact exam questions. They are approximate practice questions I collected from different sources, both official and unofficial. They help you prepare, but they do not replace studying the official theory.

I passed this exam, and so did my friends, with the help of this app.

The app works without an internet connection. Questions stay in the original English. Choose Russian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, or German, and a question mark shows the translation next to each line.

What you can do

• Study 379 practice questions in order and continue later from the same question
• Review only the questions you missed
• Take a 60-question practice test (the app marks 48 out of 60 as a pass)
• Read road signs by category, with the same translations
• Light, dark, or system appearance

No account is required. Your progress stays on your device.
```

## Play Review Notes

> The app is an unofficial, offline study tool for the Philippine LTO theory exam. It is not affiliated with LTO. The questions are approximate practice items collected by the developer from official and unofficial sources. They are not presented as the exact exam. No account is required. Questions, translations, and road signs are bundled in the app. Study progress, language, and theme stay on the device. Feedback opens the user's email app. There are no ads and no analytics SDK.
